═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   COMMON MISTAKES (DO NOT USE THESE):
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ NEXT_PUBLIC_URL                    (wrong - missing BACKEND_)
❌ NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND                (wrong - missing _URL)
❌ NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL                (wrong - different name)
❌ BACKEND_URL                        (wrong - missing NEXT_PUBLIC_)
❌ SUPABASE_URL                       (wrong - missing NEXT_PUBLIC_)
❌ next_public_backend_url            (wrong - lowercase)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   QUICK FIX STEPS:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to: https://vercel.com/dashboard
2. Select your project
3. Settings → Environment Variables
4. Delete any incorrectly named variables
5. Add 4 variables with EXACT names above
6. Check all environments (Production, Preview, Development)
7. Save
8. Go to Deployments → Redeploy latest

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   VERIFY VARIABLES ARE SET:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After deployment, open browser console (F12) and run:

console.log({
  backend: process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_URL,
  wallet: process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_TREASURY_WALLET,
  supabase: process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL
})

Should show your values, NOT "undefined"!
