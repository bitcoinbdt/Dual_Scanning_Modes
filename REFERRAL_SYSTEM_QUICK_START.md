# 🎁 Referral System - Quick Start Guide

## What You Get

A complete tiered referral system where users earn bonus credits when their referrals make purchases.

## Bonus Structure

| Package | Purchase | Your Bonus |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Starter | 50 credits | **5 credits** (10%) |
| Basic | 100 credits | **15 credits** (15%) |
| Pro | 200 credits | **40 credits** (20%) |
| Premium | 500 credits | **125 credits** (25%) |

## How It Works

1. **Share Your Code**
   - Get unique code (e.g., `ABC-DEF-GH12`)
   - Share link: `yourapp.com?ref=ABC-DEF-GH12`

2. **Friend Signs Up**
   - They use your code
   - Linked to your account

3. **Friend Buys Credits**
   - They make first purchase
   - You get bonus instantly ⚡

4. **Track Your Earnings**
   - View all referrals
   - See total credits earned
   - Monitor pending referrals
